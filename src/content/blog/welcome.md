Welcome August 26, 2021 · One min read Yimin Qiu Helper & learner Docusaurus blogging features are powered by the blog plugin . Simply add Markdown files (or folders) to the blog directory. Regular blog authors can be added to authors.yml . The blog post date can be extracted from filenames, such as: 2019-05-30-welcome.md 2019-05-30-welcome/index.md A blog post folder can be convenient to co-locate blog post images: The blog supports tags as well! And if you don't want a blog : just delete this directory, and use blog: false in your Docusaurus config. Tags: facebook hello docusaurus
